30-DAY CLIENT ACQUISITION SYSTEM

How do I find my first freelance client?

ClientES is a guided web app for beginner freelancers and service-based side hustlers. Work through 30 practical activities to decide what to offer, find potential clients, and write your first outreach message.

Get accessSee how it works

What you’ll work on

01

Decide what to offer

Choose a problem you can solve, define a small service, and prepare a sample of your work.

02

Find potential clients

Look at real examples and identify people or businesses your service could help.

03

Practice outreach

Draft a relevant message, track what happens, and prepare for conversations about scope and price.

Each activity gives you an objective, three questions, and practical next steps. Save your work as you go and use it in later activities. Days 1–5 unlock in sequence. From Day 6, each new day opens after the previous activity is complete and at least 24 hours have passed.
