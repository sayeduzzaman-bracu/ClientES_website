ESSENTIAL QUESTIONS

Before you decide

Do I need an existing client?

No. You can work with genuine examples, public research, and clearly labeled practice situations. You never have to invent customers or results.

What happens during the first five activities?

You identify skills you already have, explore possible customers and their problems, look for real clues, and choose one direction to investigate. You can complete these first five activities in sequence without waiting a full day between them.

Why do later activities unlock gradually?

From Day 6 onward, you must complete the previous activity and wait at least 24 hours before the next one opens. The pace is designed to leave room for real preparation, research, and practice instead of rushing through the whole system.

Do I have to contact clients every day?

No. Some activities focus on researching, preparing examples, practicing conversations, and reviewing decisions. Contact real people only when it is appropriate and permitted.

How much time should I spend on an activity?

It depends on the work. Some questions are quick, while research, making samples, and real-world actions may take longer. You can save drafts and continue later.

Do I need a paid AI subscription?

No paid AI subscription is required for the core activities. AI guidance is optional, and prompts are prepared for you to copy manually into a compatible AI tool.

Why use ClientOS instead of free AI prompts?

ClientOS gives you an ordered sequence of activities, saved workspaces, progress tracking, relevant earlier context, and optional task-specific prompts. The system organizes the process; you still make decisions and do the real work.

Does ClientOS guarantee clients or income?

No. ClientOS supports preparation, action, and review. Whether someone hires you depends on many factors outside the product.
